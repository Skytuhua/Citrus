# Citrus Code of Conduct

People of all backgrounds and levels of experience are welcome to participate in Citrus. Help keep issues, pull requests, reviews and other project spaces respectful and useful.

## Working together

- Discuss the behavior or change, and support technical disagreements with evidence.
- Give people room to ask questions, correct mistakes and learn the project.
- Respect other people's time, boundaries, names and identities.
- Share only files, logs and personal information you have permission to disclose.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, deliberate intimidation, repeated personal attacks and publishing another person's private information without permission are unacceptable. Retaliating against someone for raising a concern is also unacceptable.

These standards apply in project-managed spaces and when someone is representing Citrus in another community. They do not make the maintainer responsible for moderating unrelated private interactions.

## Report a concern privately

Email **[leo@citrous.app](mailto:leo@citrous.app)**. The project maintainer handles this inbox. Include relevant links, what happened, and any context needed to understand it. Share only necessary details; do not post sensitive reports in public issues.

Reports are handled with care, and identifying details should be shared only when needed to assess or address the concern. Complete confidentiality or a particular response time cannot be guaranteed. You may ask for an update or provide corrections through the same address.

If a concern involves the maintainer, or you prefer to report a violation of GitHub's own policies directly, use [GitHub's abuse-reporting process](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam). That process is separate from project moderation.

Security vulnerabilities follow the private route in [Security](SECURITY.md). Ordinary bugs and feature requests belong in [Issues](https://github.com/Skytuhua/Citrus/issues/new/choose).

## How concerns are handled

The maintainer considers the available context, impact and any repeated behavior, and may ask for clarification. Responses can include explaining the expected conduct, requesting a correction, removing disruptive content, warning a participant, or restricting participation temporarily or permanently. Serious harm may require an immediate restriction rather than a sequence of warnings.

Participants may request reconsideration by emailing the reporting address with relevant facts. Reports and moderation decisions should not be used to expose private information or encourage public retaliation.
