Citrus offline media engine

Citrus distributes FFmpeg/FFprobe and replaceable FFmpeg DLLs under the GNU Lesser
General Public License version 2.1 or later. LAME is a separate shared encoder
library under LGPL 2 or later. Ogg, Vorbis, Opus and libvpx are linked into the
FFmpeg DLLs under their BSD-style licenses. Copyright notices and full license
texts are in notices/media-licenses. Sources are pinned in media-lock.json.
No GPL or nonfree FFmpeg components are enabled. No software H.264 encoder is
included. Media Foundation H.264 is an optional operating-system encoder,
enabled by Citrus only after a successful local encode with -hw_encoding 0.

The same-release Citrus-ThirdParty-Sources.zip contains
source/citrus-media-source.tar.gz with actual source trees,
original SHA256-verified source archives, patches, build scripts, configuration,
toolchain versions, build log, headers and import libraries corresponding to
these binaries. MinGW-w64 winpthreads is a separately bundled permissive runtime;
its binary/package hashes are pinned, and its full copyright/license notices
are included, together with GCC runtime exception notices. Source delivery does not depend on a future source offer.
See SOURCE-DOWNLOADS.md for the exact download URL and checksum, and
docs/media-engine.md for rebuild and DLL replacement instructions. Source paths
in those archived instructions refer to the separate source archive.

You may modify and replace these LGPL libraries. Citrus imposes no restriction
on reverse engineering for debugging such modifications. Keep ABI-compatible
DLLs beside media/ffmpeg.exe and media/ffprobe.exe. Citrus invokes these programs
out of process; its managed application is not statically linked to FFmpeg or
LAME. End-user replacement binaries are not silently overwritten or downloaded.
